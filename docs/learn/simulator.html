    About this model

    What it is. A classroom model written in JavaScript that runs in your browser. Each study draws 600 people in 30 sites of 20, with age, true weight and systolic blood pressure, where pressure rises 0.130 mmHg per kg of true weight. Recorded weight equals true weight plus the chosen error.

    The five methods. Trust the recorded value fits the analysis on recorded weights. Audited records only drops everyone else. Fill in without the recorded value is the package's default, catmech_reference(). Fill in from the recorded value is the recommended start, catmech_reference(use_recorded = TRUE), with 20 completed copies pooled by Rubin's rules. Every record checked is a benchmark that no real study has.

    How it was checked. The filling-in and pooling follow the package's steps and were compared with the R package on 140 simulated studies at 25% and 5% audits. Least squares, one imputation draw with fixed random numbers, the pooling of the package's own completed copies, and the 18 studies where the audit found no disagreement all matched to rounding error. The random imputations matched the package's on average, within simulation noise. See docs/learn/qa/cross-check.txt.

    What it leaves out. The package's error model, catmech_contamination(), its clustered option, cluster-robust intervals, and audits selected on the true weight itself, which the package's simulations found harmful (74.0% coverage for linear imputation). Its numbers are not the package's validation results, which the guide summarizes.
