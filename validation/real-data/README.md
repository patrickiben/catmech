# Real-data pools

Two public sources, retrieved on 2026-11-01, with URLs, filters and hashes in `fetch-manifest.json`. `tools/real-data-fetch.py` and `tools/real-data-prepare.R` rebuild them. A fresh download of the registry data will differ.

The MIT license in this repository covers its code and documents. It does not cover these data, which keep their own terms. The summaries below say what those pages said when read on 2026-11-02, and they are not legal advice. Read the pages themselves before reusing the files.

**NHANES.** `nhanes-pool.csv`, and `docs/audit-planner/qa/example-pilot-nhanes.csv`, which holds 300 of its rows, come from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey 2017-2018 public-use files DEMO_J, BMX_J, WHQ_J and BPX_J, from the National Center for Health Statistics, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. The pool keeps nonpregnant adults aged 20 and over with complete analysis values, 4,884 of 9,254 participants, and selects and renames columns. It keeps the survey's participant number, `seqn`. The authors of this package did not collect these data, and NCHS did not take part in this work. The [NCHS Data User Agreement](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html) applies: use the data for statistical reporting and analysis only, make no attempt to learn anyone's identity, do not link the data with individually identifiable data, and do not try to assess disclosure protections.

**ClinicalTrials.gov.** `ctgov-pool.csv` and `validation/sponsor-clustering/sponsor-map.csv` come from the [ClinicalTrials.gov API](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies), for interventional studies with results and a completion date from 2015 through 2022, with 34,084 of 35,494 records kept after the exclusions in the manifest. Source: ClinicalTrials.gov, a database of the U.S. National Library of Medicine, with the data processed by ClinicalTrials.gov as of the retrieval date above. Changes made: fields were selected and derived as `tools/real-data-fetch.py` defines (such as the recorded and reference counts, duration, sites and phase), records were split into development and evaluation sets, and `sponsor-map.csv` adds a normalized sponsor key to the registry's lead sponsor names, with one name that was a person's email address replaced by a placeholder. The [ClinicalTrials.gov Terms and Conditions](https://clinicaltrials.gov/about-site/terms-conditions) ask anyone distributing the data to attribute the source, keep the data current, display the processing date and state any modifications, and not to assert proprietary rights or use email addresses from the database for marketing. These files are a frozen snapshot kept to reproduce the studies here, so they are not kept current. For current data, use the registry. The registry's terms also say the data carry an international copyright outside the United States, and that some may be subject to third-party copyright.

The ClinicalTrials.gov values are entered by sponsors. Neither source is a clinical source-data audit. The two real audits are described separately in [`../real-audit/README.md`](../real-audit/README.md).
